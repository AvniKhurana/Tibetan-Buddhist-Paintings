About Traditional Thangka Artwork

Safeguarding Himalayan Visual Wisdom

Traditional Thangka Artwork is an educational platform dedicated to preserving the historical iconography, natural mineral chemistry, and mathematical geometry of Himalayan scroll art.

From the ancient Newar Paubha painters of Kathmandu Valley to the monastic studios of Lhasa and Mustang, Thangkas are painted meditations. Our articles document the exact specifications used to depict deities such as the Wheel of Life (Bhavachakra), Medicine Buddha, Green and White Tara, Mahakala, and Yamantaka.
